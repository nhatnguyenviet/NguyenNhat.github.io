## A tiny bit of theory

A metal-oxide gas sensor changes its resistance when gas molecules land on its surface. The response is often written as

$$ S = \frac{R_a}{R_g} $$

where $R_a$ is the resistance in air and $R_g$ is the resistance in the target gas. Smaller nanostructures mean more surface per volume, so a bigger response.
